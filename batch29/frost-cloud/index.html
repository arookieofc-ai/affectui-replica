Floats on a sky gradient, glances around on its own, blinks (sometimes twice), follows your pointer, and winks when you click or press it. Adapts to light and dark themes.

How to test.
Move your pointer over the cloud — its eyes follow. Leave and it resumes wandering. Click, tap, or press Enter on the cloud to make it wink and squish. Toggle sky, color, and follow with the buttons above.
Replica notes.
Geometry, palette, gradients, filter widths, and timing constants match the demo data verbatim. Motion is painted with in-place SVG transforms each frame — no re-renders. Honors reduced-motion and pauses off-screen.
Clean-room replica for study purposes · single file · no external assets.
